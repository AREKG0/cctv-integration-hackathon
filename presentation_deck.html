PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Core Challenge in Gujarat Public Safety

1. Heterogeneous Infrastructure

26 independent departments operating standalone VMS platforms, mixed analog/IP cameras, and different video retention periods (7 to 15+ days).

2. Geographical Dispersion

Camera sites distributed over 1,000 km ranging from border districts to Valsad, Dahod, Somnath, Jamnagar, and Dwarka.

3. Fragmented Databases

Government databases (VAHAN, SARTHI, eGujCop, NAFIS) operate in silos without automated video cross-referencing.

4. Slow Criminal Search

Law enforcement currently spends hours manually reviewing isolated video feeds to trace stolen vehicles.

SYSTEM DESIGN

Hybrid Innovative Architecture (Model 5)

MODEL 1: GIS REGISTRY

Dynamic discovery via /api/ingest, camera health monitoring, and coverage gap analysis.

MODEL 2: UNIFIED VIEWING

Low-latency WebRTC (WHEP) preview grid and RTSP stream feed aggregation.

MODEL 3: VMS FEDERATION

Adapter plugin framework, RabbitMQ event bus, and cross-system correlation.

MODEL 4: CENTRAL AI

PyTorch/YOLOv8 ANPR engine, PostGIS spatial route reconstruction, and watchlist alerts.
